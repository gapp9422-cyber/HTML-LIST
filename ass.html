THIS IS HOW I MAKE MY STIR FRY PASTA AND CHICKEN

Step 1
Add the Pasta to boiling water till it's semi done
Step 2
On another side of the burner;add groundnut oil to a pre heated pan
Step 3
Add in onions to the already heated oil and let it fry till it turns golden brown
Step 4
Add in already roughly blended pepper mix into the fried oil and leave your heater on low heat;add the seasoning, and the spices together with the ginger and garlic paste
Step 5
while that is frying,deep fry your chicken in a separate pan with groundnut oil to taste
Step 6
Add the fried chicken to the frying pepper mix and onions;stir together gently
Step 7
Sieve the water out from the pasta
Step 8
Add the sieved Pasta to the fried pepper mix after removing the chicken
Step 9
Stir gently on low heat
Step 10
Your Delicious stir fry pasta and Chicken is ready
